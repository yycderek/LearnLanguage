# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Independent language learners, teachers, and self-directed course authors. Learners use it in short daily sessions; authors use Studio at a desk to shape private or shareable courses.

## Product Purpose

LearnLanguage lets people learn any language from structured courses and create those courses without code. Success is a learner reaching the next useful language action while retaining ownership of their courses and progress.

## Positioning

A local-first, open language-learning and course-authoring platform: the learning engine, language packs, Course Packs, and clients stay separate, so one language or client does not define the product.

## Operating Context

Learn is the focused learning space: course choice, plan, lesson, review, and progress. Studio is the authoring space: course creation, import, preview, language-pack management, and publishing. Users may work fully offline and without an account.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Local-first Web/PWA with optional AI and optional self-hosted sync.
- Chinese and English interface/instruction languages; target languages remain independent of the interface language.
- Built-in and imported Course Packs, local backup/restore, and deterministic review.
- Learn should support motivating progress feedback without rankings, paywalls, artificial scarcity, or forced social competition.
- Studio remains a calm professional workspace; game mechanics belong only to Learn.

## Brand Commitments

LearnLanguage is clear, practical, learner-respecting, and non-patronizing. It does not pretend that a streak or score is language mastery.

## Evidence on Hand

Real built-in course packs and language packs live in `@learn-language/content`. No customer testimonials, rankings, or external learning-outcome claims are available; the interface must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Any language, one dependable learning loop.
- Ownership before lock-in.
- Progress should feel rewarding and remain evidence-based.
- Learn and Studio are distinct jobs with distinct interfaces.
- Optional intelligence must never block deterministic learning.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard use, visible focus, Chinese/English content, touch access, reduced motion, and readable mobile text are product requirements.
